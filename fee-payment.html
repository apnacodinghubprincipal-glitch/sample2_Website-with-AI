ONLINE FEE PAYMENT SYSTEM

Considering the various payment methods including fees, it has been realised that managing a team of 5000 parents going for various type of routing payment through out the year, online payment system has been sorted out in collaboration with our Bankers "BOI Agrasain Branch" and their online payment service provider "SabPaisa.in"

As per this facility digital e-transaction based school fees management programme as on optional mode is being offered to parents/students to make essential payment timely thereby avoiding crowding on school cash counters or in the bank. Thus using the online payment system parents:

  1. Can pay at anytime from anywhere at lighting fast speed.
  2. Instant safe and secure payment gateways and Receipt generation will ensure mental peace.
  3. It is a time saving readily available payment system accessible from anywhere and will completely eliminate standing in line for payments.

It is hoped that parents will enjoy the convenience of online payment gateway w.e.f. 1st September, 2020.

• NOTICE - Please keep a copy of the fee payment receipt as hardcopy or softcopy for future reference. Kindly put the ID No. very carefully, if mistaken the amount will be credited in some other student A/C.
