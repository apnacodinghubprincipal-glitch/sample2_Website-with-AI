Facility Details

Designed for Excellence

4 Science Labs

Separate labs for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science with ISO-certified equipment.

2 Libraries

Bar-coded system with 15,000+ books, periodicals, newspapers, and a digital reading section.

3 Computer Labs

100+ computers with high-speed internet. Compulsory computer education from Class III.

3 Dance Rooms

Fully equipped dance studios for classical, contemporary, and folk dance training.

Music Studio

Modern synthesizers, sitars, tablas, and a dedicated practice room for budding musicians.

Medical Room

On-campus nurse with first-aid facilities. Regular health check-ups for all students.
