Starting the Day with Grace

Daily Morning Prayer

Dear God,
Motivate us to be your worthy children,
Motivate us to obey our parents, teachers,
Elders and neighbours.
Make us sober, responsible and dutiful,
Inspire us to become helpful, generous and
Sympathetic to all your creations.
Bestow wisdom, health, prosperity
Upon us and our fellow beings.
Bless our parents, brothers and sisters,
Friends, dear teachers, neighbours and relatives
Shower your choicest blessings on everyone at
Agrasain Balika Siksha Sadan.
Keep us away from hatred, anger,
Greed and all other vices.
O' Our Father in Heaven, bless in a special way to all
Those who have put in their might and hardwork
To make our Institution what it is now.
Thank You Father for giving us an
opportunity to study in this wonderful school.

Agrasain Balika Siksha Sadan
