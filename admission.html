How to Apply

Admission Process

Simple, transparent steps to join the ABSS family.

Submit Application

Fill out the online application form or collect a physical form from the school office. Attach required documents.

Entrance Assessment

Applicants appear for an age-appropriate assessment. For Nursery & KG, an interaction session is conducted.

Confirmation

Selected students receive an offer letter. Complete fee payment and document verification to confirm enrollment.

Frequently Asked Questions

The child must be at least 3 years old as of 1st April of the academic year for Nursery admission.

For Nursery and KG, there is an informal interaction session. For Classes I and above, a written assessment in English, Mathematics, and relevant subjects is conducted.

Birth certificate, previous school report card, transfer certificate (if applicable), 4 passport-size photographs, Aadhaar card copy of the student, and address proof.

No, transport has to be arranged by the parents. School does not provide transport service.
