Our Programs

Nurturing Minds, Building Futures

From playful early years to rigorous senior academics — a seamless journey of growth.

Early Years (Nursery – KG)

Our early years program provides a warm, nurturing environment where little ones learn through play, exploration, and guided discovery. We follow a carefully designed curriculum that builds foundational skills while keeping the joy of childhood alive.

  • Play-based learning methodology
  • Phonics and early literacy programs
  • Number sense and pre-math activities
  • Art, music, and movement sessions
  • Social-emotional development focus
  • Safe, colorful, child-friendly classrooms
Enroll Now

Primary School (Class I – V)

Building strong academic foundations while encouraging curiosity and creativity. Our primary program follows the ICSE framework enriched with hands-on projects and experiential learning.

  • ICSE curriculum with enrichment activities
  • English, Hindi, Bengali language programs
  • Mathematics with practical applications
  • Environmental Science & Social Studies
  • Computer education from Class III
  • Regular assessments and parent communication
Enroll Now

Middle School (Class VI – VIII)

A bridge between foundational learning and subject specialization. Students develop critical thinking, research skills, and deeper subject understanding.

  • Advanced ICSE curriculum
  • Dedicated science laboratories
  • Introduction to specialized subjects
  • Project-based and collaborative learning
  • Leadership and public speaking programs
  • Career guidance and aptitude workshops
Enroll Now

Senior Secondary (Class IX – XII)

Rigorous academic programs preparing students for competitive exams, higher education, and professional careers. ISC board with multiple stream options.

  • ISC board – Science, Commerce, Arts streams
  • Advanced lab work and research projects
  • Competitive exam preparation support
  • Career counseling and university guidance
  • Internship and community service programs
  • National & international olympiad coaching
Enroll Now
