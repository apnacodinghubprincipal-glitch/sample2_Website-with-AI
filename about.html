From the Principal's Desk

A Warm Welcome

Principal's Welcome Note

Head of School, Agrasain Balika Siksha Sadan

Dear Parents and Students, welcome to Agrasain Balika Shiksha Sadan. For over three decades, we have been committed to nurturing young girls into confident, compassionate, and capable women.

Our school is built on the belief that every child is unique, and education must adapt to bring out the best in each one. We blend traditional values with modern teaching methodologies, ensuring our students are prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

At ABSS, we don't just educate minds — we shape characters. From our award-winning science program to our vibrant arts curriculum, every aspect of our institution is designed to foster holistic growth.

I invite you to explore our campus, meet our dedicated faculty, and discover why ABSS is the trusted choice for thousands of families across Howrah and beyond.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Our Journey

A Legacy of Excellence

From humble beginnings to becoming a nationally recognized institution.

1994 — Foundation

ABSS was established with a vision to provide quality education for girls in the Liluah area, starting with just 500 students.

2000 — CISCE Affiliation

Received affiliation from the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations for ICSE and ISC programs.

2008 — ISO Certification

Became one of the first schools in the region to receive ISO 9001:2008 certification for educational excellence.

2015 — National Recognition

Produced multiple national toppers in ICSE examinations, establishing ABSS as a center of academic brilliance.

2024 — Modern Campus

Expanded infrastructure with smart classrooms, advanced labs, and upgraded sports facilities serving over 2000 students.
